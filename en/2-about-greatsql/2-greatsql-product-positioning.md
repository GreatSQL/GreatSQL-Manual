# Product Positioning
---

The GreatSQL database is an **open source and free** database that can meet financial-grade application scenarios on commodity hardware. It features **High Availability**, **High Performance**, **High Compatibility**, and **High Security**, and can serve as an ideal drop-in replacement for MySQL or Percona Server for MySQL.

The GreatSQL database is widely supported and applied in a variety of business application scenarios, including but not limited to the following industries/business scenarios:
- Government and public services, e-government systems, data analysis, etc.
- Internet and technology industries, e-commerce, social networks, content management, etc.
- Financial services industry, banking systems, payment platforms, insurance businesses, etc.
- Telecommunications industry, customer management systems, billing systems, etc.
- Energy industry, smart grid systems, oil and gas industry systems, etc.
- Education industry, online education platforms, school management systems, etc.
- Healthcare industry, hospital information management systems, medical research, etc.
- Retail and logistics industry, inventory management, customer relationship management (CRM), etc.
- Media and entertainment industry, streaming media services, gaming industry, etc.
- Manufacturing industry, production management systems, supply chain management, etc.
- Logistics and transportation industry, transportation management systems, warehouse management, etc.
- Travel and hospitality industry, online travel booking systems, hotel management systems, etc.

Application scenarios and cases for GreatSQL can be found in almost every industry, thanks to its high availability, high performance, flexibility, scalability, and broad ecosystem support.
