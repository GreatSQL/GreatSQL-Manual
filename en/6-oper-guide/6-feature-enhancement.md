# Operations Feature Enhancements

---

This section introduces the operations feature enhancements of the GreatSQL database.

Compared with MySQL, GreatSQL adds many operations feature enhancements, such as the user statistics feature, the ProcFS plugin, compressed columns with dictionaries, extended InnoDB engine status display, extended grant display, improved handling of corrupted tables, and many newly added `INFORMATION_SCHEMA` tables, etc.
