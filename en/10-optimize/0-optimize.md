# Performance Optimization
---

This article mainly introduces multi-level optimization references from hardware, operating system to GreatSQL.

As a very important link in application business systems, database performance greatly affects the response efficiency and user experience of business systems. Therefore, great attention is paid to performance optimization both in application-side development and in the database itself.

Follow good SQL application development standards, do a good job of database performance optimization, avoid the database becoming the bottleneck of the business system, improve system stability and reliability, and bring greater value to the business.
