## Performance Benchmark Methods

This article mainly introduces several different methods for benchmarking GreatSQL performance, mainly including:

1. [sysbench Benchmark](./3-1-benchmark-sysbench.md)
2. [BenchmarkSQL Benchmark](./3-4-benchmarksql.md)
3. [TPC-H Benchmark](./3-2-benchmark-tpch.md)

Different stress testing solutions can be selected and customized according to the actual situation and business needs.
